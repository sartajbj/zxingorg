Generate a Dragon Ball Legends QR Code Online

A DBL QR code is a scannable image made from your Dragon Ball Legends friend code. This free DBL QR Code Generator turns your friend code, or up to three at once, into clean QR images you can download and share directly in your browser, no account and no app required.

What Is a DBL QR Code Used For?

Dragon Ball Legends uses friend-code QR scanning for several in-game features: adding friends without typing a long code, Legends Fest and anniversary events that ask you to scan friend QR codes for Chrono Crystals, and Shenron or Porunga summon events where scanning codes from other players unlocks rewards.

How to Create Your DBL QR Code

Find your friend code from Menu → Friend → Profile Info, paste it into the generator, and click Generate DBL QR Code. Add up to two more codes if you want to generate several at once, then download each QR as a PNG image.

How to Scan a DBL QR Code In-Game

In Dragon Ball Legends, go to Menu → Friend → Invite Friend, then choose Scan Code. Point your camera at the QR image on another screen, or select a saved QR image from your photos if scanning directly isn't available.

Troubleshooting a QR Code That Won't Scan

Double-check that the friend code is exactly 8 characters with no extra spaces. Make sure your screen brightness is high enough for the scanning camera to read the code clearly, and avoid screenshotting a QR code from a compressed image, since this can blur the fine detail. Downloading the PNG directly from this generator avoids that problem.
