Frequently Asked Questions

What is ZXing Online Decoder?

ZXing Online Decoder is a free browser-based tool that scans and decodes QR Codes and Barcodes without installing any software.

Is ZXing Online Decoder free to use?

Yes. You can scan unlimited QR Codes and supported barcode formats completely free.

Does ZXing Online Decoder upload my images?

No. Your QR Code images are processed inside your browser whenever possible, helping keep your data private.

Which barcode formats are supported?

ZXing Online Decoder supports QR Code, Data Matrix, PDF417, Aztec, Code 128, EAN-13, UPC-A, ITF, Codabar and many other formats.

Can I decode Wi-Fi QR Codes?

Yes. Wi-Fi QR Codes are decoded with network name, password and security type displayed in an easy-to-read format.

Can I scan QR Codes using my phone camera?

Yes. Open Camera to scan live or upload a QR Code image from your gallery.

Can ZXing Online Decoder read damaged QR Codes?

If the QR Code is still readable, the decoder will attempt to recover and decode the information successfully.

It offers a fast, clean and privacy-first experience with support for QR Codes, Wi-Fi QR Codes, URLs, contacts, email, phone numbers and many barcode formats.
