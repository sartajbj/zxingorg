About ZXing Online Decoder

ZXing Online Decoder is a free browser-based tool that lets you decode QR codes and many popular barcode formats instantly without installing any software. Simply upload an image, paste a QR code from your clipboard, or use your device camera to scan and decode information securely in seconds.

What Is ZXing?

ZXing (Zebra Crossing) is one of the world's most trusted open-source barcode scanning libraries. It supports QR Codes, Data Matrix, PDF417, Aztec, EAN, UPC, Code 128, Code 39 and many other barcode formats. Developers and businesses around the world rely on ZXing because of its speed, accuracy and reliability.
