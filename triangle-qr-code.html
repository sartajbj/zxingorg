Create a Triangle QR Code Online

A triangle QR code is a stylized QR code designed to give the familiar square QR pattern a more distinctive visual appearance. This free Triangle QR Code Generator lets you create a triangular-style QR code from a URL, text, Wi-Fi details, email address, phone number or SMS message directly in your browser.

What Is a Triangle QR Code?

A triangle QR code uses triangular visual elements as part of its design while preserving the fundamental QR code structure required for detection and decoding. This makes the design useful when you want a QR code that looks different from a conventional square pattern without removing the information encoded in the QR.

How to Create a Triangular QR Code

Choose the type of information you want to encode, enter your content and select your preferred QR colors and error-correction level. Click Generate Triangle QR Code to create the result. You can then download the design as PNG or SVG for use on websites, digital graphics, documents and other projects.

Triangle QR Code vs. a Standard QR Code

A standard QR code normally uses a square grid with square modules. A triangular QR design changes the visual treatment of the modules while retaining the underlying QR data structure. For practical scanning, keeping sufficient contrast, clear spacing and the important finder patterns is essential.

What Can You Encode?

You can create a triangle QR code for many common types of data, including website URLs, plain text, Wi-Fi connection information, email addresses, phone numbers and SMS messages. For more advanced QR generation options, you can also use our full QR Code Generator .

Keep Your Triangle QR Code Scannable

QR styling should never come at the expense of readability. Use strong contrast between the QR and its background, avoid placing important graphics over the code and leave adequate white space around the outside. For a conventional QR workflow, you can also use the ZXing online decoder to scan supported QR codes and verify their encoded content.
